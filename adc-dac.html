Dr. Seán Mullery · Atlantic Technological University. Quantisation and aliasing: the analogue signal is computed exactly at each sample instant; quantisation statistics use 20 000 samples, each second taken at a different sampling phase. Sampling and reconstruction: the analogue world is simulated at 2048 samples/s over a 4 s record, after Steven W. Smith, The Scientist and Engineer's Guide to DSP, ch. 3. Butterworth, Chebyshev and Bessel filters run as causal analogue filters, so their delay, overshoot and ringing appear as they would in hardware; the ideal filter is applied in the frequency domain and is non-causal.
